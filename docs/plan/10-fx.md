# 10 — Chaos VFX: hits, pots, tricks, combos

## 1. Purpose

Every ball-ball hit, cushion hit, pot, trick shot, combo and scratch gets a loud, pooled, cheap effect, on every peer, at
the moment its ball is drawn there (user ask 2026-10-12: "as much chaos as possible"). **Presentation only**: it reads what the
host already decided and never writes `Shot`, `Cash`, `Effect` or physics state. No audio (out of scope this pass).

## 2. Concepts & data model

### 2.1 Where each peer gets its events

| FX | Host (solo = host) | Client |
|---|---|---|
| ball-ball, cushion, cue strike | `Ball.contacted` (+ `PhysicsSim.shot_started` for the strike) at the real moment | `PhysicsSim.frame_received(frame)` → `FxHitTap` decodes the event stream → `PhysicsSim.at_playback(tick, fn)` |
| pot, scratch, penalty | `Ball.net_flag(ball, POCKETED)` | `PhysicsSim.ball_flag_applied(ball, POCKETED)` (already gated on the playback clock) |
| trick | `RoundDirector.trick_detected` | same signal, re-emitted at `end_tick` by `_shot_resolved_rpc` |
| streak / shot bookkeeping | `PhysicsSim.shot_started_net`, `RoundDirector.shot_ended` | same, every peer |

A client has no contacts, only the host's contact events (04 §2.4: velocity after every impact). `FxHitTap` (pure, `src/gameplay/fx/fx_hit_tap.gd`)
rebuilds them: an event whose velocity jumps by ≥ `MIN_DV` (0.35 m/s, horizontal) against the previous event dead-reckoned with
`BallMotion.advance` is an impact, except that the cue ball's first event of a Shot is its strike, a **kick** before anything else is asked (a cue ball resting beside a cushion, in a pocket's jaw zone or touching a ball still pops, and a strike into the cushion pops then rails). Otherwise a same-tick neighbour within `2R + slack + 3 ticks of relative travel` → **ball-ball**, strength = relative
pre-impact speed (RoundDirector's bomb uses the same number); else near a cushion (≤ 4R) and not at a pocket → **rail**, strength = approach
speed into the cushion; else the cue ball's later lone jump (nudge, blast) → **kick**. Pocketed balls, lifecycle flags and vertical hops never hit.
Host and client agree on the *set* of FX and their timing (≤ playback delay). The host re-sends a corrected velocity one tick after a cushion bounce
(`Ball._rail_rebound`): that echo is dropped (`ECHO_TICKS`), but a ball-ball hit in the same few ticks can read stronger on the client. Jaw bounces at a pocket pop on neither
(the host's `contacted` reports cushions only; the client drops lone bounces within `POCKET_REACH_RADII` of a pocket).

### 2.2 Strength → size

`t = clamp(strength / 8 m/s, 0, 1)` (`ImpactFx.HARD_MPS`; the cue's power cap is 20 since adhoc-1013c W2, so a full break hits at up to 20 m/s and every hit past 8 is full size). `t` scales pop size (`4–10 R` ball, `3–7 R` rail, `3.5–8 R` strike), spark
count (`amount_ratio` 0.35–1.0), the flash light (0.8–3.5) and decides whether a word ("CLACK! CRACK! WHAM! SMACK!") floats up (ball `t > 0.55`, rail `t > 0.7`, at most
one per 160 ms). `R` = `TableSpec.ball_radius_m`; no metre literal.

## 3. Rules / formulas

### 3.1 Combo and streak (`FxCombo`, presentation only)

| rule | value |
|---|---|
| scoring pot | a `POCKETED` ball whose `RoundDirector.value_of(type) > 0`; counts only while a Shot is live |
| zero-value pot | bursts, no count; negative value (the black) = "OUCH!" fizzle, no count |
| `n` | pots so far in this Shot + completed streak (consecutive Shots with ≥ 1 pot and no foul) |
| `level` | `clamp(n − 1, 0, 8)`; scales pop size (`9 + 2·level` R), fountain amount, light, shake (`0.12 + 0.05·level` trauma), colour (`COMBO_COLORS[level]`) |
| announce | `n ≥ 2`: banner "COMBO xN" + `!` per two levels, colour flash, 3D label; `level ≥ 4` adds confetti |
| streak break | a Shot with no pot, or a foul, resets it; a streak of ≥ 2 shows "STREAK xN LOST" and a fizzle |
| scratch | cue ball `POCKETED`: grey fizzle + "SCRATCH..." (no banner; the foul and fine are 02's) |

### 3.2 Trick shots

`trick_detected` is sorted best first (01 §3): the first one per `shot_id` is celebrated, the rest ignored. `tier = clamp(round((mult − 1) / 0.15), 1, 4)` (Combo 1,
BankShot 1, KickShot 2, Combo3 2, Carom 2, RailRunner 4). **A trick celebrates only when it pays** (adhoc-1012a playtest: Carom fired on ~60 % of a bot's shots, most of them potting
nothing, and "CAROM! PAYS x1.35 +$0" with confetti on every second shot made the show mean nothing): `FxCombo.shot_pots == 0` → `trick_minor`, a small title on the minor row and a floating label, no
"PAYS", no confetti, no shake. A paid trick = trick banner (`COMBO SHOT!`, `BANK SHOT!`, `KICK SHOT!`, `CAROM!`, `TRIPLE COMBO!!`, `RAIL RUNNER!!!`) + "PAYS xM", a colour flash, a floating label over the table, a flash light and
`0.35 + 0.12·tier` trauma; confetti rain from tier 2, a cannon out of each bottom corner from tier 3.

**The show never sits on the aim.** While the local player is in CUE mode (`Player.mode`), `FxStage` ghosts the banners and confetti to `AIM_FADE` (0.15) opacity within 0.15 s and brings them back when the shot leaves
the cue (playtest: 60 % of shots started aiming with a banner over the pack they were aiming at).

**Pops grow with distance** (`ImpactFx._far_scale`): the camera stands at the near rail and a 0.3 m pop at the far end is a speck, so pop size scales by `distance / POP_REF_M (2 m)`, clamped to `1..POP_FAR_MAX (2.6)`.

### 3.3 Tiers and budgets (07 §2)

| | tier 0 | tier 1 | tier 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| particle `amount_ratio` / pool share | 1.0 | 0.6 | 0.35 |
| spawned hits per frame | 6 | 4 | 2 |
| flash light, screen colour flash | on | on | off |
| shake | full | half | half |

Pools are built once in `ImpactFx._ready` and never grow: 8 spark + 4 fountain + 2 fizzle `GPUParticles3D` (24–120 one-shot dots each), 12 pop quads (`fx_pop.gdshader`, one
instance-uniform driven additive quad, no light), 6 `Label3D`, one shared `OmniLight3D` (the third realtime light of 07 §2; no shadow), and in `FxStage` (a `CanvasLayer`, layer 3) a
flash `ColorRect` (`fx_flash.gdshader`, additive, **no** screen-texture read, so it is not a second post pass), 3 banner `Label`s and 3 `GPUParticles2D`. Idle = no `_process`.
Camera shake is `Camera3D.h_offset / v_offset` (≤ 0.12 m at full trauma, restored to 0 when it settles); it never touches the camera's transform.
Measured by `tests/fx/fx_cost.gd` (dev PC, RTX 3050 Ti, 1280×720, worst-case storm: 4 hits every frame + a pot per 0.4 s + a trick per 2.5 s):

| | GPU ms | draw calls | primitives |
|---|---|---|---|
| table at rest | 0.129 | 18 | 4,724 |
| storm, tier 0 | 0.232 (+0.10) | 55 (+37) | 7,186 |
| storm, tier 1 | 0.228 (+0.10) | 46 (+28) | 7,014 |
| storm, tier 2 | 0.148 (+0.02) | 38 (+20) | 6,518 |

Real play is far below the storm (pooled nodes are idle between events). Phone numbers: not measured (needs `m0_device_run.sh`).

### 3.4 The game's own beats (adhoc-1012a playtest)

The playtest found whole moments of play that said nothing: a hazard started silently at GO LIVE, a Drink went down with no word, the Round began and ended without a beat, and the Pocket Jackpot paid one pocket the crew
could not see. `ImpactFx` now listens to the siblings that decide them (`EventScheduler`, `EffectManager`, `GameLoop`, found by path; a probe harness has none) and answers on every peer:

| beat | source | show | `fx_fired` |
|---|---|---|---|
| a hazard starts | `EventScheduler.event_applied` | trick row `<NAME>!` + minor row `HAZARD - PAYS x1.25 / NEXT POT PAYS x2 / ONE POCKET PAYS x3 / NEW RULES`, flash, shake 0.45 | `hazard` |
| a Drink lands on this peer | `EffectManager.live_changed` (new `live_drinks()` entry) | minor row `<DRINK>!` in its `DrinkTier` colour, small flash | `drink` |
| a Round starts | `GameLoop.round_started` | combo row `ROUND n - GO!`, green flash | `go` |
| a Round ends | `GameLoop.round_ended` | `cleared`: `TABLE CLEARED!`, confetti tier 3, flash, shake; `timer`: `TIME!`; `shots`: `ROUND OVER` | `cleared` / `time` |
| Pocket Jackpot | `event_applied` + `EventScheduler.active_pocket` | a pulsing gold ring and `x3` on the paying pocket until the Round ends; a pot there reads `JACKPOT!` with confetti | `jackpot` |
| Flaming Shot (`flames` visual hook) | `EffectManager.visual_hook_started` | every hit with `t > 0.15` adds a fire-coloured pop, an ember fountain and an orange flash light (the hook had no consumer) | `flames` |

All of it uses the existing pools (the banner rows, one pop quad and one `Label3D` for the jackpot ring, the fountain / light slots); `tests/fx/fx_cost.gd` is unchanged by it (storm tier 0: +0.11 ms GPU, +37 draws).

### 3.5 The aim guide (adhoc-1013c U13)

Not an impact effect but the one FX element that is drawn *before* the shot: while the local player is in CUE the cloth shows the cue ball's path to its first contact, a ghost ring where it touches, the struck ball's line (orange, arrow) and one more stretch past the contact (the bounce off the cushion, the cue ball past the ball), curved for a massé, an arc for a jump. What it computes and its tolerances are 01 §3 "Shot guide"; the look is `ShotGuide`: ribbons 12 mm wide, 4 mm above the cloth, warm white `#FFEE8C` (the same family as the pop quads' sparks, readable on the green felt and the dark skins), cyan where the ball is off the cloth, red when the stroke miscues, orange `#FF9433` for the object ball, fading with length; previewed at 55 % opacity until a charge starts. Local shooter only (puppets' cues carry none, no spin on the wire); it lives on its own render layer (12, `ShotGuide.LAYER`): the local camera draws it, the mirror's passes do not, and a feed camera (W4's tripod / ceiling feeds) must leave it out of its cull mask; hidden the moment the shot fires, so it never competes with the hit pops. Budget: one `ImmediateMesh` (unshaded alpha, a few hundred vertices), **+1 draw call and no process cost outside CUE**, rebuilt only when an input changes; it is a plain mesh, not a particle or a post pass, so `fx_cost.gd` is untouched. The FX banners ghost to 15 % while the player is in CUE (3.2) so the show never sits on the guide either.

### 3.6 Round staging — the saucer and the pit (adhoc-1013c U15/U16, 06 P33 A4)

The two sequences that open and close a Round are FX by this document's rules (presentation only, the same picture on every peer from data the peer already has, budgeted) and by two more of their own. **No collision object, ever:** the saucer, its beam, the landing pad, the vent, the flaps, the pit and the ghost balls own no `CollisionObject3D`/`CollisionShape3D` (`staging_probe` walks the nodes), so a sequence can never snag a player or move a ball; the fixtures that are floors or lids (`Trapdoor`'s panels, `Table`'s lid) say so in 01/04. **Nothing in the host's physics waits on a picture:** the balls exist, frozen, from the first frame and are only hidden and revealed; the clean-up never moves a real ball (a client's replica would write it back), it hides it and drops a stand-in.

*Materialise (`Materialise`, `materialise.gdshader`).* One additive overlay (`GeometryInstance3D.material_overlay`) plus the instance `transparency`, driven by a per-instance `progress` 0..1: the mesh fades in over the first 70 % while a cyan scan band climbs it and a fresnel shell with scan lines covers what is not there yet; at 1 the overlay is removed and the transparency is exactly 0, so the mesh is back on the opaque pipeline unchanged. It rides on top of whatever material the mesh wears, so every ball type (the gimmicks, the stone, the cue ball) and a shop or skin that changes a ball's material need nothing of their own. The same call dematerialises the saucer.

*Saucer (`Ufo`).* A flattened sphere, a glass dome, a torus of 16 chasing rim lamps (`ufo_rim.gdshader`, unshaded), an underside glow, a tractor-beam cone (`ufo_beam.gdshader`, additive, open ends, stripes turning and running up it) and a landing pad on the cloth under the beam: 6 draw calls, no light, no shadow. The beam is world-space so it can point at any ball; it rests on ball *i* while *i* materialises and glides to *i + 1*. Order, times and the saucer's flight are `StagingPlan`: pure functions of the clock and the ball count (the reveals share a 2.2 s window, at most 0.3 s a ball, so the set-up never passes 4.75 s).

*Pit (`TablePit`, `StagingTray`, `staging_tray.gdshader`).* The bed is 8 hinged flaps cut from the cloth mesh (positions, normals, tangents and UVs interpolated at the cuts, the cloth's own material and colour), dropping open middle first with a gravity-like ease; a ball rides its flap until the flap has tilted 14°, then falls at 8 m/s² into an unlit box (a warm dark at the lip, black at the bottom) and fades as it sinks; the flaps close eased out. 2.3 s, constant for any ball count. The mirror's window pass draws the flaps and the pit (it draws the table), so the table in the mirror opens too.

## 4. Godot implementation sketch

`ImpactFx` (one node in `MainLevel.tscn`, exports `round_director`, `table`) → `fx_hit_tap.gd` (`FxHitTap`), `fx_combo.gd` (`FxCombo`), `fx_presets.gd` (`FxPresets`: node builders, generated textures),
`fx_stage.gd` (`FxStage`), shaders `src/shaders/fx_pop.gdshader`, `fx_flash.gdshader`. `ImpactFx.fx_fired(kind, strength)` fires once per FX spawned (probes, a future debug HUD). Tests: `tests/fx/fx_probe.gd`,
`fx_pair_probe.gd`; hand tools `fx_gallery.gd` (PNGs of each FX), `fx_cost.gd` (the table above).

## 5. Interfaces to other modules

Reads: `PhysicsSim.{shot_started, shot_started_net, ball_flag_applied, frame_received, at_playback, playback_tick, active_cue}`, `Ball.{contacted, net_flag, pre_solve_velocity, motion}`, `RoundDirector.{trick_detected, shot_ended, value_of}`,
`Table.{pockets, cloth_y, spec}`, `QualityManager.current_tier`. **`PhysicsSim.frame_received(frame: PackedByteArray)`** is one new signal, emitted at the top of `apply_frame()` (client path);
the client FX have no contact stream without it (`fx_pair_probe` fails loudly when it is missing). Writes: nothing outside its own nodes.

## 6. Open questions

- A reduce-motion / screen-shake option in Settings (accessibility): shake is `ImpactFx._shake`, one place to gate.
- Audio for the same events is out of scope; `fx_fired` is the hook.
- Cushion hits right at a pocket mouth are dropped on the client (jaws), host `contacted` reports only cushions: parity is by zone, not by body.

## 7. MVP cut vs later

Built: everything above. Later: stretched spark streaks (billboard + align-to-velocity needs a custom particle shader), per-BarSkin colour grading of the flash, a combo meter on the HUD.

## 8. Playtest (adhoc-1012a)

`src/tools/playtest.gd` plays a whole solo Night of the real `MainLevel` unattended and judges whether it is chaotic and fun. It needs a window (it screenshots), not `--headless`:

```bash
godot --path . -s src/tools/playtest.gd -- --tag=run --seed=7 --nights=1 [--out=DIR] [--rounds=N] [--shots=N] [--skill=0.6] [--spend=0.5] [--power=1.0] [--wild=0.2] [--slots=3] [--vote=<event id>]
python3 src/tools/playtest_compare.py DIR before after > compare.md   # mean (min..max) per metric over playtest_<prefix>_s*.json
```

It presses Solo, then each Intermission votes the Event card, buys at the bar, pulls the slot and walks to GO LIVE through `WorldInteractions` (teleporting the player stands in for walking); each Round it picks a ghost-ball shot and plays it through the player's own `CueController` (`enter_cue`, `turn`, `set_spin`, `release_power`), so nothing bypasses the real path. Deterministic per `--seed`; compare runs over several seeds, one window at a time (two windows share the GPU and inflate every frame-time number). `--vote=tilted_table` takes that card whenever the Round's offer holds it, the offered and picked ids are in each Round's notes. `settle_s` per shot is the creep tail: the last frame a ball moved faster than 0.3 m/s to the shot resolving. Output per tag: `playtest_<tag>.json`, `summary_<tag>.md`, `sheet_<tag>_{typical,best,dull}.png` (thumbnail strips of a shot), `aims_<tag>.png`, UI screenshots of the vote, bar, slot and GO LIVE.

| target | how it is measured | goal |
|---|---|---|
| every shot shows FX | `ImpactFx.fx_fired` count per shot | `shots_any_fx_pct` = 100 |
| feedback fast | strike to first `fx_fired` | median and max ≤ 0.3 s, no late shots |
| no dead stretch | seconds a ball moves faster than 0.8 m/s since the last FX (creep below that is reported apart) | worst ≤ 3 s |
| combos / tricks | `FxCombo` combo or a trick per Round | ≥ 1 per Round on average |
| chaos | Drink, Event, slot chaos or zap per Round | ≥ 1 per Round |
| slot | lever to result | ≤ 3 s |
| aim stays clear | `FxStage` ghost alpha 0.35 s into the aim | no opaque FX over the cue line |
| frame budget | render GPU p95 and draw calls inside shots | 07 (`fx_cost.gd` unchanged) |
