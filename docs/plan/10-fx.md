# 10 — Chaos VFX: hits, pots, tricks, combos

## 1. Purpose

Every ball-ball hit, cushion hit, pot, trick shot, combo and scratch gets a loud, pooled, cheap effect, on every peer, at
the moment its ball is drawn there (user ask 2026-10-12: "as much chaos as possible"). **Presentation only**: it reads what the
host already decided and never writes `Shot`, `Cash`, `Effect` or physics state. No audio (out of scope this pass).

## 2. Concepts & data model

### 2.1 Where each peer gets its events

| FX | Host (solo = host) | Client |
|---|---|---|
| ball-ball, cushion, cue strike | `Ball.contacted` (+ `PhysicsSim.shot_started` for the strike) at the real moment | `PhysicsSim.frame_received(frame)` → `FxHitTap` decodes the event stream → `PhysicsSim.at_playback(tick, fn)` |
| pot, scratch, penalty | `Ball.net_flag(ball, POCKETED)` | `PhysicsSim.ball_flag_applied(ball, POCKETED)` (already gated on the playback clock) |
| trick | `RoundDirector.trick_detected` | same signal, re-emitted at `end_tick` by `_shot_resolved_rpc` |
| streak / shot bookkeeping | `PhysicsSim.shot_started_net`, `RoundDirector.shot_ended` | same, every peer |

A client has no contacts, only the host's contact events (04 §2.4: velocity after every impact). `FxHitTap` (pure, `src/gameplay/fx/fx_hit_tap.gd`)
rebuilds them: an event whose velocity jumps by ≥ `MIN_DV` (0.35 m/s, horizontal) against the previous event dead-reckoned with
`BallMotion.advance` is an impact. Same-tick neighbour within `2R + slack + 3 ticks of relative travel` → **ball-ball**, strength = relative
pre-impact speed (RoundDirector's bomb uses the same number); else near a cushion (≤ 4R) and not at a pocket → **rail**, strength = approach
speed into the cushion; else the cue ball leaving rest → **kick** (the cue strike). Pocketed balls, lifecycle flags and vertical hops never hit.
Host and client agree on the *set* of FX and their timing (≤ playback delay). The host re-sends a corrected velocity one tick after a cushion bounce
(`Ball._rail_rebound`): that echo is dropped (`ECHO_TICKS`), but a ball-ball hit in the same few ticks can read stronger on the client. Jaw bounces at a pocket pop on neither
(the host's `contacted` reports cushions only; the client drops lone bounces within `POCKET_REACH_RADII` of a pocket).

### 2.2 Strength → size

`t = clamp(strength / 8 m/s, 0, 1)` (`ImpactFx.HARD_MPS`; the cue's power cap is 10). `t` scales pop size (`4–10 R` ball, `3–7 R` rail, `3.5–8 R` strike), spark
count (`amount_ratio` 0.35–1.0), the flash light (0.8–3.5) and decides whether a word ("CLACK! CRACK! WHAM! SMACK!") floats up (ball `t > 0.55`, rail `t > 0.7`, at most
one per 160 ms). `R` = `TableSpec.ball_radius_m`; no metre literal.

## 3. Rules / formulas

### 3.1 Combo and streak (`FxCombo`, presentation only)

| rule | value |
|---|---|
| scoring pot | a `POCKETED` ball whose `RoundDirector.value_of(type) > 0`; counts only while a Shot is live |
| zero-value pot | bursts, no count; negative value (the black) = "OUCH!" fizzle, no count |
| `n` | pots so far in this Shot + completed streak (consecutive Shots with ≥ 1 pot and no foul) |
| `level` | `clamp(n − 1, 0, 8)`; scales pop size (`9 + 2·level` R), fountain amount, light, shake (`0.12 + 0.05·level` trauma), colour (`COMBO_COLORS[level]`) |
| announce | `n ≥ 2`: banner "COMBO xN" + `!` per two levels, colour flash, 3D label; `level ≥ 4` adds confetti |
| streak break | a Shot with no pot, or a foul, resets it; a streak of ≥ 2 shows "STREAK xN LOST" and a fizzle |
| scratch | cue ball `POCKETED`: grey fizzle + "SCRATCH..." (no banner; the foul and fine are 02's) |

### 3.2 Trick shots

`trick_detected` is sorted best first (01 §3): the first one per `shot_id` is celebrated, the rest ignored. `tier = clamp(round((mult − 1) / 0.15), 1, 4)` (Combo 1,
BankShot 1, KickShot 2, Combo3 2, Carom 2, RailRunner 4). **A trick celebrates only when it pays** (adhoc-1012a playtest: Carom fired on ~60 % of a bot's shots, most of them potting
nothing, and "CAROM! PAYS x1.35 +$0" with confetti on every second shot made the show mean nothing): `FxCombo.shot_pots == 0` → `trick_minor`, a small title on the minor row and a floating label, no
"PAYS", no confetti, no shake. A paid trick = trick banner (`COMBO SHOT!`, `BANK SHOT!`, `KICK SHOT!`, `CAROM!`, `TRIPLE COMBO!!`, `RAIL RUNNER!!!`) + "PAYS xM", a colour flash, a floating label over the table, a flash light and
`0.35 + 0.12·tier` trauma; confetti rain from tier 2, a cannon out of each bottom corner from tier 3.

**The show never sits on the aim.** While the local player is in CUE mode (`Player.mode`), `FxStage` ghosts the banners and confetti to `AIM_FADE` (0.15) opacity within 0.15 s and brings them back when the shot leaves
the cue (playtest: 60 % of shots started aiming with a banner over the pack they were aiming at).

**Pops grow with distance** (`ImpactFx._far_scale`): the camera stands at the near rail and a 0.3 m pop at the far end is a speck, so pop size scales by `distance / POP_REF_M (2 m)`, clamped to `1..POP_FAR_MAX (2.6)`.

### 3.3 Tiers and budgets (07 §2)

| | tier 0 | tier 1 | tier 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| particle `amount_ratio` / pool share | 1.0 | 0.6 | 0.35 |
| spawned hits per frame | 6 | 4 | 2 |
| flash light, screen colour flash | on | on | off |
| shake | full | half | half |

Pools are built once in `ImpactFx._ready` and never grow: 8 spark + 4 fountain + 2 fizzle `GPUParticles3D` (24–120 one-shot dots each), 12 pop quads (`fx_pop.gdshader`, one
instance-uniform driven additive quad, no light), 6 `Label3D`, one shared `OmniLight3D` (the third realtime light of 07 §2; no shadow), and in `FxStage` (a `CanvasLayer`, layer 3) a
flash `ColorRect` (`fx_flash.gdshader`, additive, **no** screen-texture read, so it is not a second post pass), 3 banner `Label`s and 3 `GPUParticles2D`. Idle = no `_process`.
Camera shake is `Camera3D.h_offset / v_offset` (≤ 0.12 m at full trauma, restored to 0 when it settles); it never touches the camera's transform.
Measured by `tests/fx/fx_cost.gd` (dev PC, RTX 3050 Ti, 1280×720, worst-case storm: 4 hits every frame + a pot per 0.4 s + a trick per 2.5 s):

| | GPU ms | draw calls | primitives |
|---|---|---|---|
| table at rest | 0.129 | 18 | 4,724 |
| storm, tier 0 | 0.232 (+0.10) | 55 (+37) | 7,186 |
| storm, tier 1 | 0.228 (+0.10) | 46 (+28) | 7,014 |
| storm, tier 2 | 0.148 (+0.02) | 38 (+20) | 6,518 |

Real play is far below the storm (pooled nodes are idle between events). Phone numbers: not measured (needs `m0_device_run.sh`).

## 4. Godot implementation sketch

`ImpactFx` (one node in `MainLevel.tscn`, exports `round_director`, `table`) → `fx_hit_tap.gd` (`FxHitTap`), `fx_combo.gd` (`FxCombo`), `fx_presets.gd` (`FxPresets`: node builders, generated textures),
`fx_stage.gd` (`FxStage`), shaders `src/shaders/fx_pop.gdshader`, `fx_flash.gdshader`. `ImpactFx.fx_fired(kind, strength)` fires once per FX spawned (probes, a future debug HUD). Tests: `tests/fx/fx_probe.gd`,
`fx_pair_probe.gd`; hand tools `fx_gallery.gd` (PNGs of each FX), `fx_cost.gd` (the table above).

## 5. Interfaces to other modules

Reads: `PhysicsSim.{shot_started, shot_started_net, ball_flag_applied, frame_received, at_playback, playback_tick, active_cue}`, `Ball.{contacted, net_flag, pre_solve_velocity, motion}`, `RoundDirector.{trick_detected, shot_ended, value_of}`,
`Table.{pockets, cloth_y, spec}`, `QualityManager.current_tier`. **`PhysicsSim.frame_received(frame: PackedByteArray)`** is one new signal, emitted at the top of `apply_frame()` (client path);
the client FX have no contact stream without it (`fx_pair_probe` fails loudly when it is missing). Writes: nothing outside its own nodes.

## 6. Open questions

- A reduce-motion / screen-shake option in Settings (accessibility): shake is `ImpactFx._shake`, one place to gate.
- Audio for the same events is out of scope; `fx_fired` is the hook.
- Cushion hits right at a pocket mouth are dropped on the client (jaws), host `contacted` reports only cushions: parity is by zone, not by body.

## 7. MVP cut vs later

Built: everything above. Later: stretched spark streaks (billboard + align-to-velocity needs a custom particle shader), per-BarSkin colour grading of the flash, a combo meter on the HUD.
