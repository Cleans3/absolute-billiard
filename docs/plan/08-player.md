# 08 — Player v1 (touch-first)

*Ruling R1 (Cleanse, 2026-09-30): Player is built now, touch first, desktop parity. Supersedes the "Player = black box" line in 00/05 for everything below; gamepad stays deferred.*

*Update (06 P15 / P18, 2026-10-02): **cosmetics and avatar animation are no longer deferred.** Every player is a jointed `Humanoid` in a `CharacterSkin` (§2.6, §2.7) that walks, leans over the cue, and goes limp on a hit; the "no animation rig in v1" and "Cosmetics" lines below are superseded. The Skins screen (main menu, `src/ui/SkinsScreen.tscn`) lets a player equip one; with nothing equipped a player wears the skin of their seat. Everything is free and unlocked while the game is in test mode.*

## 1. Purpose
Turns a person into two things the systems already consume: a `ShotIntent` for 01 when it is their Shot, and a position + `Interactable` use for 03/02 while someone else shoots.
Phones first: every control is reachable with two thumbs, every hot path fits 07's `_process` budget.

## 2. Concepts & data model

### 2.1 Body and camera
`Player` (`CharacterBody3D`, capsule `r 0.3 m`, `h 1.75 m`, eye `1.6 m [tune]`) — first person, yaw on the body, pitch on `Head` only (the split already in `player.gd`). Collision **layer 4 `world`**, mask `2 | 4` (table rails + world): players walk around the table, never through it, and balls (mask `1|2|3`, 07 §1) never test against a player. Table bumping is a `WorldInteraction`, not a collision.

### 2.2 Modes
| mode | when | camera | input |
|---|---|---|---|
| `WALK` | default; **seated** is a sub-state of it (below) | first person | move + look + interact |
| `CUE` | you are `current_shooter_id`, balls at rest, you tapped **Shoot** within `cue_reach_m` of the cue ball | pinned behind the cue ball at eye height, looking along the aim line; orbits the ball as the aim turns | aim, power, spin, elevation, call-pocket |
| `CARRY` | you picked up a `CameraRig` (`camera_grab`) | first person, rig held in front | walk + look; **Place** drops it (`camera_place`) |
| `RAGDOLL` | knocked (§2.7) for `2 s`, then `0.4 s` of get-up blend | rides the ragdoll's head bone | none: move, look, interact and cue input are ignored |

`RAGDOLL` is entered from any other mode: `CUE` is left exactly like **Back** (position, yaw and pitch restored), `CARRY` asks the host to put the rig down at your feet (`place_camera(true)`, the usual path and cooldown; if the host refuses, you are still the holder and `CARRY` resumes when you stand). It ends in `WALK`.

`CUE` exits on strike, on **Back** (position, yaw and pitch restored), or when `current_shooter_id` changes. **A strike keeps the player where they shot** (06 P27, C16): `exit_cue(false)` leaves the pinned spot, snapped to the nearest standing position that fits — the aim spot itself, else backing away along the shot line (`0.1 m` steps, up to `3 m`), else rings around it — found with a capsule query on the player's own mask (walls, bar, other players) and kept outside the table outline including its wooden rails (`table.spec`: half extent + rail width + capsule radius); yaw and pitch stay as aimed, and Back, a shooter change, host-away and a knock still restore the spot from before aiming. If the host refuses the shot (`shot_rejected`, inline on the host, over the wire on a client) the player is walked back to that spot. `player_probe` checks the stay, the snap, the late and the inline refusal. Entry is refused with a toast when it is not your turn, balls are moving, or the camera is farther than `cue_reach_m` (3.5 m at table scale 2) from the cue ball. In `CUE` the **camera** is pinned `BACK_M = 1.2 m [tune]` behind the cue ball on the aim line (yaw = aim, pitch fixed to look at the ball); no collision runs. The **body is not**: the table is wider than an arm, so it stands at the nearest floor point outside the table's outline and leans over the rail to the stick (§2.7 "CUE body", 06 P33 U1). Only the local slot ever builds a camera, HUD or `TouchControls`; remote slots render an avatar.

**Sitting (06 P33 U7).** `WALK` has a `seated` flag, not a fifth mode (the pose's mode bits are full). The bar stools (counter x 4.3 and the six round-table stools) and the two lounge sofas (two seats each) are `Interactable`s of the local kind `sit` (`Bar.SEATS`, prompt *Sit*, never sent to the host). **Use** sits: `Player.sit_on` snaps the player to the seat's floor point and facing, disables the capsule (the collision exception: a seated body is nobody's obstacle, the stool's own box is not in its way), takes the `SIT` stance (§2.7) and lowers the camera to the seated eye (hip on the seat + the same rise, eased `3 m/s`: a stool `1.55 m`, a sofa `1.21 m`). Look still turns the swivelling body; the counter's Bartender stays usable from a stool; with nothing in reach the prompt offers *Stand up*. **Up:** Jump, a push on the stick or a movement key (> `0.3`), *Stand up*, a knock, a `kick_stool` anyone does, entering `CUE`, or any `GameLoop` phase change; the player stands where they stood before sitting, else on the nearest spot that fits (`_free_spot`, the same search a strike uses). The flag rides the pose (§2.6).

### 2.3 Touch layout (`TouchControls`, a `CanvasLayer` child of `UILayer`)
Shown when `DisplayServer.is_touchscreen_available()`; hidden on desktop. All targets `≥ 48 dp`, sized through the project's `canvas_items` stretch (base `1280×720`, aspect `expand`). A **mirror** setting swaps left/right for left-handed players.

| mode | left thumb | right thumb | buttons |
|---|---|---|---|
| `WALK` | floating stick: origin where the finger lands in the left half, radius `80 dp`, dead zone `0.15` | drag-look anywhere in the right half (the LookPad covers exactly that half, under the buttons), `0.20 °/px [tune]` | **Interact** (label = the nearest `Interactable`'s prompt, hidden if none in reach) · **Shoot** (only while you can strike: a Round is live, it is your turn, every ball at rest, nothing being staged; `Player.can_shoot_now`, re-read `10 Hz`: 06 P33 U6) · **Jump** (an up-arrow icon, `icon_jump`, no text) |
| `CUE` | spin disc (`120 dp`, bottom corner) and a cue-angle tile above it (`120×72`): both only **show** their value, and a **tap opens the enlarged control** (§2.8: hit point, cue angle `0–80°`, each holds its value) | drag anywhere else = aim yaw `0.12 °/px`, a second finger down = fine aim `×0.25` · power slider on the right edge: drag down from its top to set power, release to strike, drag back under `3%` to cancel | **Back** (top corner) · pocket markers (top row) (only when `rule_call_pocket > 0`: tap one of the 6 before the power slider unlocks) |
| `CARRY` | stick | look | **Place** · **Drop** (Interact hides: Place is the same action) |

Every mode also shows a **Pause** button (`64 dp`, top corner opposite Back, i.e. top-right unless mirrored). In `CUE` the LookPad covers the whole screen under the widgets. Sensitivity, FOV, invert-Y and the mirror come from `Settings` (§2.8).

### 2.4 Desktop parity
Mouse captured in `WALK`/`CARRY`: `WASD` move, mouse look (`0.0025 rad/px`), `F` interact (`interact`), `Tab` shoot / back (`cue_mode`), `Space` jump. In `CUE`: mouse X aims, hold the existing `shoot` action (right mouse button) charges power (the existing rise-then-bleed curve), release strikes, `I/J/K/L` spin, `Q/E` elevation, `C` resets spin, number keys `1–6` call a pocket. Same `ShotIntent` either way — 01 never learns the device (P1).

### 2.5 `ShotIntent` (01 §2) — one field added
`called_pocket: StringName` (`&""` = none). Required by 03's `rule_call_pocket`; the host scores a pot into any other pocket as `0`, not a foul (03 §5).

### 2.6 Remote avatars
A remote slot renders the same `Humanoid` the local player wears (§2.7), in the `CharacterSkin` from its roster entry's `char_skin` (the seat's `seat_red|seat_blue|seat_yellow|seat_green` when none is equipped; `SkinCatalog.resolve_character`; `src/resources/skins/characters/`), a `Label3D` name tag (billboard, `fixed_size`), and the cue stick mesh while that peer is in `CUE` — 2 draw calls for the body, 1 for the tag, the stick extra. In `CUE` the avatar is on the same rig as its owner (§2.7 "CUE body"): `0.2 m` left of the aim line, leaning over the rail, both hands on the stick; in `CARRY` both hands hold the rig in front of it. The puppet's stick is the fixed one (at rest on the ball, on the aim line: spin, elevation and pull-back are not on the wire), placed in the world and on the mirror layer; the name tag follows the head. Poses come from `PlayerSlot` at `10 Hz` (04 `push_player_pose`) and are interpolated `100 ms` behind, like balls. A pose is 16 B: `pos 3×f32 | yaw u16 | u16 = mode << 14 | seated << 13 | pitch (13 bit, ±π/2)`; the mode is `0 WALK · 1 CUE · 2 CARRY · 3 RAGDOLL`; `seated` (06 P33 U7, only with mode WALK) makes the puppet take the `SIT` stance on the seat at its position (it finds the nearest `sit` `Interactable` for the seat height). The pitch field lost one bit (resolution `0.02°`, was `0.01°`); the packet is still 16 B, so `push_player_pose` and the 04 §5 table are unchanged. The puppet renders the sample bracket at `now − 100 ms` (a newer sample is reached, never jumped to). While `RAGDOLL` the owner sends its **pelvis** XZ as the position and the puppet ignores the stream's position (it is limp on its own local physics); see §2.7 for how it stands again.

**Backgrounding (04 §6):** `PlayerSlot` sends no pose while `NetManager.backgrounded`; on `host_away_changed(true)` `TouchControls` shows *Host away, waiting…*, the Player leaves `CUE` and refuses to enter it until the host is back. The shot-clock text also clears at `round_ended`.

### 2.7 Humanoid, ragdoll and knocks (06 P15)
**Body.** `Humanoid` (`src/gameplay/player/humanoid.gd`, a `Node3D` that rests facing −Z with its feet on the origin) is built in code: 11 bones — pelvis, torso, head, upper/lower arm ×2, thigh/shin ×2 (hands and feet are skinned to the forearm and shin) — and **two skinned meshes with one shared vertex-coloured material**: the body (trunk **with a lid on it**, shoulders, both arms, legs, shoes) and the head (head, face, hat, neck). `apply_skin(CharacterSkin)` bakes `body_color` (torso, upper arms), `accent_color` (legs, shoes darker, cap), `skin_color` (head, hands, forearms) and the hat (`none|cap|tophat|beanie|crown`) into the vertices; it rebuilds ~550 triangles and leaves the skeleton alone. **First person (06 P26, C17).** The local player calls `set_first_person(true)`: only the head surface moves to render layer 20, which the local `headCam` culls (it still casts its shadow, and the mirror camera draws it, §2.9), so looking down you see your own **torso, arms and legs**, never your head and never the inside of your body — the trunk is closed by a lid, and **the head leans forward over the chest as you look down** (`Player._lean_eye`, local only): the `Head` pivot sits `0.12 m` ahead of the trunk's axis standing and glides to `0.28 m` ahead (`0.05 m` lower) between a `50°` and an `89°` look-down, so the camera clears the lid (which at eye level is 10 cm below the lens and filled the screen as one flat plane) and sees the chest sloping away, both arms hanging with their hands, the thighs and the shoes beyond; the eye stays inside the capsule (`r 0.3`) so it never pokes through a wall (the pivot, not the camera node: a camera child would swing the offset down with the pitch). Shallower than about `50°` the body is below the view's lower edge, which is physics of a `75°` field of view, not a bug. The earlier arrangement (arms on the culled surface, trunk open at the top) was dropped: arms are now on the body mesh, so the avatar is still 2 meshes and 5 draw calls (07). `ragdoll_probe` fails unless the local camera renders arms, lid and legs, and does not render the head. In `CUE` the local body is hidden from its own camera the way the head is: its mesh moves to `HEAD_LAYER` (the leaned trunk and arms would fill the aiming view), so the mirror's window pass and every other camera still draw it and it keeps its shadow (06 P33 U1; the earlier shadow-only trick hid it from the mirror too, a floating head). `ragdoll_probe` and `mirror_probe` check the layers. The one shared material is `src/shaders/humanoid.gdshader`: vertex colour × `0.214` linear (the old `albedo × 0.5` sRGB, because the room's key lights are tuned for dark wood) plus a fill — emission `0.9 ×` the albedo and a flat `0.03` linear floor — so the side of a body the lamps miss reads as dim colour and near-black trousers are not a hole (a dim room made the look-down body and the mirror image black silhouettes). `ragdoll_probe` checks that at no pitch from 0 to 89° any point of the trunk's lid is in the camera's frustum and that at 80°+ the chest front and both hands are.

**Animation** (all procedural, `_process`, ~19 µs per body): breathing; a gait driven by the node's own measured horizontal speed (`stride 4.5 rad/m`, thigh swing `±0.45 rad`, knee flex up to `0.85 rad`; the pelvis rides the straighter leg so a foot is always on the floor; a teleport above `9 m/s` counts as zero speed); the head follows `Head` pitch for players and `look_at_point` for the bartender; `set_hand(side, point)` reaches a hand to a point with two-bone IK. Stances: `STAND`, `CUE` (torso leaned `0.65 rad`, legs split, hands at the stick: without the rig below, rear grip `(0, 0.957, −0.28)`, bridge `(0, 0.957, −0.78)`), `CARRY` (hands at `(±0.14, 1.10, −0.40)`) and `SIT` (06 P33 U7: thighs level on the seat, hip joint `0.075 m` above the seat surface, shins hanging, hands on the thighs; `set_seat(top)`), selected by `Player.mode` and `Player.seated`.

**CUE body (06 P33 U1, A2).** The camera stays where the shooter aims from; the body adjusts to the table and the real stick, and is visible in the mirror. `CueController._process` poses the stick (tip with the spin offset, elevation, pull-back) and then calls `Player.pose_body(tip, butt_dir, length, bridge_s)` → `Humanoid.set_cue_rig`; a puppet does the same from its fixed stick each frame (`Player._puppet_cue`). **Feet:** `CueStance.feet_for` finds the nearest point on the aim line behind the ball where the pelvis axis (`0.2 m` left of the line so the stick passes the right shoulder) is `BODY_R_M 0.3 + CLEAR_M 0.25` outside the table's outline (cloth plus the wooden rails, `CueStance.outline_half`), no nearer than `BACK_M`: under the camera when that is already clear, else back along the line (bisection). The humanoid node moves there and keeps the aim yaw; no leg IK, the legs are the stance's FK (thigh swing right `−0.25`, left `0.30`; knees `0.08` / `0.45` plus up to `0.7` as the trunk bends, which lowers the pelvis). **Trunk and hands:** `Humanoid._solve_rig` tries trunk pitches from `0.65` in `0.05` steps and takes the least with both hands where they want to be — the rear hand under its shoulder inside the last `0.15–0.6 m` of the stick (butt grip), the left hand at the bridge `BRIDGE_M 0.36 m` behind the tip plus the pull-back (the bridge hand stays put while the stick slides) — within an arm's reach (`UPPER + FORE − 0.02`) and never closer than `0.25 m` to its shoulder (a stick passing the shoulder at a low elevation would put the hand in it); past `1.0 rad` it stops looking for the wanted spots, bends no less, and **a hand that cannot reach its spot slides along the stick to the nearest point it can** (the hand sticks to the cue, which the oversized table makes common for a ball in the middle), deepening toward `1.25 rad` only while an arm still falls short; an arm that cannot reach the stick at all stretches straight (`rig_gap`). Both arms are the existing two-bone IK (`set_hand` path). The head keeps looking at the ball. `tests/player/cue_stance_probe.gd` runs a ball grid × 8 yaws: no hip, leg or foot below the rail top inside the table's footprint, the pelvis axis `0.55 m` outside the outline, and where the solver says an arm reaches, the skeleton's hand is within 5 cm of the stick; plus the local `enter_cue → CueController → pose_body` path (full power, 40° elevation) and a remote puppet's pose. What cannot be done: a ball too far from every rail (the table is `2×` regulation, an arm and a trunk lean reach about `1.1 m` past the pelvis) leaves the hands bunched on the butt end of the stick — real, not hidden. **Rail lean (06 P25, C13):** `lean_on(world_point, seconds)` is a transient pose layered on any stance — the trunk pitches `0.55 rad` toward the point (about the horizontal axis across the direction, so it leans at the rail whatever way the body faces) and both hands rest on it, `0.12 m` apart — for `2 s`, or until the body walks off (`> 1 m/s`) or goes limp. It is not part of the pose blob (that stays 2 mode bits): every peer starts it on that player's body from the applied interaction (`LeanFeedback`, 03 §3.7).

**Ragdoll.** A `PhysicalBoneSimulator3D` holds 11 `PhysicalBone3D`s (box/capsule/sphere per segment, total `≈ 56 kg`) joined by cone joints (torso `25°`, head `45°`, shoulders `100°`, hips `70°`) and hinges (elbows fold forward, knees fold back, `~135°`). Bodies sit on collision layer 5 `ragdoll` with mask `2|4` (07 §1): they touch the table rails and the room, never each other and never balls. **Idle they are not simulating and are on no layer** (`animate_physical_bones = false`), so a standing player costs no physics. `ragdoll(impulse)` starts the simulation from the current pose; `impulse` is a velocity change in m/s of the whole body, split unevenly (head `1.6`, trunk and arms `1.3`, thighs `0.6`, shins `0.3`) so a shove topples instead of sliding, plus the speed the body was walking at and a random spin. A second `ragdoll()` while limp only adds a shove. `get_up()` snapshots the lying pose, stops the simulation, emits `got_up` and blends every bone from the lying pose to the animated one over `0.4 s` (smoothstep). `is_ragdoll()`, `pelvis_position()`, `eye_transform()` (head centre, a little forward) are the read side.

**`Player.knock(impulse, seconds)`** leaves `CUE`/`CARRY` cleanly (§2.2), disables the capsule and input, hands the body to physics and sets mode `RAGDOLL`; the local camera detaches (`top_level`) and rides `eye_transform()` (smoothed `25 /s`). After `seconds` the Player stands: the `CharacterBody3D` moves to the pelvis XZ (Y unchanged), input returns, the camera eases back onto `Head` over `0.4 s`, `got_up` is emitted, and a rig the host never took off us puts us back in `CARRY`. A knock while limp extends the time. Remote puppets ragdoll when the knock RPC arrives **or** the pose stream says mode 3 (whichever is first; the other is then a no-op or just adds the shove), ignore pose interpolation while limp, and stand when their own timer is out **and** the owner's latest pose is no longer mode 3 (or `3 s` after the timer, so a silent owner cannot pin them); they then resync to that pose and drop the stale ones. Each peer simulates its own ragdoll: purely cosmetic, the bodies are not synchronised.

**Triggers.**
| trigger | where | knock |
|---|---|---|
| thrown drink (`throw_drink`) | `WorldInteractions._soak_shooter`, host | the **shooter**, pushed away from the thrower (`3.5 m/s` + `1.5 m/s` hop); nobody soaks themselves |
| bomb ball blast | `RoundDirector._detonate`, host | every player within `2.5 m` (flat) of the blast, `4.5 m/s` + hop |
| stumble (Mystery debuff) | `PlayerSpawner._process`, host, 1 Hz tick | stat `player_stumble_per_min` per peer from `EffectManager` (Poisson, rate `/60` per second; Mystery drinks' `static_stumble` is 2 a minute), random direction `2.5 m/s` |
| hard landing | owner's `_physics_process` | falling faster than `7 m/s` when landing; the owner knocks itself and tells everyone (`PlayerSlot.push_player_knock`) |

The host entry point is `PlayerSpawner.knock(peer_id, impulse, seconds := 2.0)` → `apply_knock` RPC on every peer (04 §5); `knock_away(peer, from, speed)` and `knock_blast(origin)` are its helpers. The bar is `3.6 m` high, so nothing a player can reach on foot comes near `7 m/s` (a `1.8 m` drop is `5.9 m/s`): the hard-landing trigger exists for launches and effects, and the probes launch the body at the floor to exercise it.

**Skins screen (06 P18).** `src/ui/SkinsScreen.tscn` + `skins_screen.gd`, a full-screen `ui_modal` (`open()` / `close()` / `is_open()`, `closed`) the main menu opens. Two tabs, **Character** and **Bar**, each a card grid beside a preview. **Cards are names only** (polish 2 C1): the skin's name and an "Equipped" badge, no colour chips and no palette list, so the look is only ever seen in the preview. The character preview is a `Humanoid` in a `SubViewport` turning at `0.7 rad/s` in the previewed skin; the **bar preview spins too** (polish 2 C2): a pre-rendered panorama of the whole bar room in that skin (`assets/ui/bar_preview/<skin_id>.png`, 1024 × 752, a full turn from the room's middle in cylindrical projection folded into two rows, ~0.3 MB each imported) that `src/shaders/bar_preview.gdshader` slides a 108° window over, one turn per `BAR_TURN_SEC = 14 s`: smooth, no frame blending, no 3D and one texture on the phone. `src/tools/render_bar_previews.gd` renders the sheets (needs a window; `godot --path . -s src/tools/render_bar_previews.gd [-- <skin_id>]`, then `--import`) from 64 narrow strips per skin — **re-run it whenever the room changes** (decor, lightmap re-bake) or a `BarSkin` is added; a skin with no sheet shows no picture rather than an error. Tapping a card previews it, **Equip** writes `user://profile.json` at once (`SkinCatalog.equip_character` / `equip_bar`), the badge follows. "Free while in test mode" is printed in the header; there is no price, no lock, no ownership. The `bartender` skin is not offered. `tests/economy/skins_probe.gd` and `tests/player/skins_pair_probe.gd` guard it.

**Bartender (06 P24, C4).** `src/gameplay/bar/Bartender.tscn` (`Bar/Bartender` in `MainLevel`, at the stand point behind the counter, facing −X) is a `Humanoid` in the `bartender` skin and the **barkeep quirk** (`set_quirk(&"barkeep")`: an oversized head with bulging eyes and a red nose, a handlebar mustache, a very tall plum-and-gold striped hat, a red bow tie, gold sleeve garters; the same bones and the same two meshes) on a static capsule (layer 4, so players bump into him). It is not an `Interactable`; the counter props are (02 / P17). Idle life: breathing, head eased toward the nearest player within `4 m`, and every `6–12 s` the right hand wipes the counter for `3 s`. **He talks unprompted.** `BartenderChat` (a child, `bind(level)` finds `EconomyManager`, `GameLoop`, `RoundDirector`) says a line after `5–8 s` and then every `11–19 s` (sooner at an Intermission), weighted by phase and purse; `BartenderLines.POOLS` holds ten pools of 15+ lines each — `pep`, `special` (names a Drink that is on the shelf now, `{drink}`; none on the shelf → a pitch instead), `pitch`, `cash_low` / `cash_high` (purse under 35 % of the quota / at or over it), and the reactions `pos_pot`, `pos_trick`, `neg_scratch`, `neg_foul`, `neg_miss`. Reactions come a beat after the shot settles (`RoundDirector.shot_ended`, and `EconomyManager.fined` for a scratch): a scratch and a trick shot always get one, a foul 75 %, a pot 45 %, a miss 55 %; one waits at a time, higher priority replaces lower, and a foul right after a scratch is the scratch's remark only. The negatives are cutting but indirect — sarcasm and backhanded compliments, never a plain insult. Lines come out of a shuffle bag per pool and skip the last 12 said, so nothing repeats back to back; reactions are picked by the shot's id, so most peers hear the same remark, while ambient lines are per-peer (cosmetic, no RPC). A line shows in a `SpeechBubble` (one `Label3D` and one rounded quad with a tail, billboarded over the hat, popping in and fading, scaled `0.5–1.5×` with the viewer's distance around `6 m` so it reads from the table and does not swallow the screen up close), is babbled by `BartenderVoice` (a blip per vowel group of the line, six buzzy tones rendered once in code, pitch from the letters, a lift before a question mark, a breath at punctuation; `AudioStreamPlayer3D` on Master, so the volume setting applies; no audio files) and is acted by an arm program per pool (`cheer`, `flourish`, `sweep`, `shrug`, `hip`: two-bone IK targets plus a head bob). `tests/player/bartender_probe.gd` checks the pools (size, length, no duplicates, `{drink}` in every special), the shelf link, no back-to-back repeats, an unprompted line with bubble and blips, and each reaction.

**Mirror (06 P26 as amended, C14, adhoc-1012a).** `src/gameplay/bar/Mirror.tscn` (`Mirror` in `MainLevel`, on the west wall, centre `(−6.90, 1.4, −3.8)`, facing +X, `3.6 × 2.4 m`, wood frame with a brass lip: one mesh) shows the room, the table and every avatar — the local player's head and hat included. Two layers, so nothing re-renders the room per frame. **Room:** `Mirror.capture()` renders six `FACE_PX` (`1024 / 768 / 512` by quality tier 0 / 1 / 2, each with mipmaps: the room is minified from a distance and shimmered without them; 06 P33 U2) cube faces from `CAPTURE_AT` (2.5 m in front of the glass) with a camera that draws only `Mirror.ROOM_LAYER` (render layer 11: the shell meshes; `tag()` puts lights, the lightmap and probes on both mirror layers so neither pass draws black) into a `Texture2DArray`; `mirror.gdshader` reflects the live eye ray off the glass, intersects the room box (`DecorPlan.HALF`, `CEIL`) and samples the face it hits — exact for the walls, a small parallax error for furniture. Captured once after load and again on `BarSkinDirector.skin_applied`; headless it is a no-op. **Avatars:** a `SubViewport` (3:2, transparent background, no MSAA, no shadow atlas; its height follows the glass on the viewer's screen — `SIZE.y × focal / distance` pixels, rounded up to 64 and clamped to `128 .. 1024 / 768 / 384` by tier, grown at once and shrunk only two steps late — so it is sharp when you stand at the glass and cheap across the room) holds a frustum `Camera3D` at the viewer's eye mirrored through the wall plane with the near plane **on the glass** and `frustum_offset` the glass's centre, drawing `Humanoid.MIRROR_LAYER` (render layer 10: avatars, the table, which sits too near the capture point to box-project, and the cue stick) and layer 20 (the local head, and the local body while it shoots, §2.7); the shader composites it over the room with U flipped. It draws **every frame** while the glass is on screen and the viewer is in front of it, **at any distance** (the old `8 m` range stopped the pass, leaving the last frame of the avatars and the table frozen in the air over the live room — 06 P33 U2), placed from `get_global_transform_interpolated()` — the old 20 Hz rate and the tick-time eye are what made the reflection swim. `Mirror.set_blood(amount)` (0..1, local, a shader uniform: splatter that spreads with the amount, one hand smear, drips running down from the frame) is for the failure cutscene (09). Measured with `src/tools/perf_mirror.gd` (real `MainLevel`, five humanoids, 11 balls, HUD; render scale 0.85, MSAA 2×; needs a window; dev PC 2026-10-12, with the adhoc-1012a textured room, at the old fixed `576 × 384` pass and 512 px faces — not re-measured for the sized pass, 07): draw calls p95 **69** facing the glass at 3.4 m, 66 at 1.8 m, 75 oblique, 88 off screen (old mirror, old room: 100 / 99 / 92 / 101); window pass (avatars + table) 0.22–0.26 ms GPU per frame. `tests/player/mirror_probe.gd` covers layers, camera geometry, the gates (no range cut-off), every-frame drawing, the sized pass per tier, the cube face sizes, `set_blood` and the shader's face table; `src/tools/screenshot_mirror.gd` shoots the glass for a visual check.

### 2.8 Screens, regions and settings (06 P11, P12)
**One Theme.** `src/ui/theme/game_theme.tres` (project setting `gui/theme/custom`) styles every Control from the SVG sprites in `assets/ui/`; scenes carry no font-size or colour overrides, they pick a type variation (`HudLabel`, `Title`, `Heading`, `Caption`, `Payout`, `ToastInfo|Good|Bad`, `PrimaryButton`, `FlatButton`, `PanelInset`, `PanelPlate`, `ChipBox`). Custom-drawn widgets read the palette from the theme's `Game` type (`get_theme_color(&"brass", &"Game")`). Regenerate: `python3 src/tools/ui_art.py`, `--import`, `godot -s src/tools/build_ui_theme.gd`.

**Modal screens (`UIRouter`, `UIModal`).** `UILayer/UIRouter` watches every Control in group `ui_modal` (`open() / close() / is_open()`, `visible` is the state). At most one is open: a higher `priority` displaces the open one, which returns when the newcomer closes; an equal one replaces it; a lower one is held back. A `GameLoop.phase_changed` closes every modal without `keep_on_phase`. The router owns `Input.mouse_mode` (free while a modal is open; captured in-game on desktop), the single toast line (`toast(text, kind)`) and Esc (closes the top dismissable screen, else opens Pause). `Player` / `CueController` ignore input while `UIRouter.blocking()`. The simulation is never paused. Priorities: PhaseScreen -10 · BartenderMenu 0 · EventVote 20 · Pause 50 · Settings / Controls / Skins 60. The Lobby scene has its own router for Settings / Controls / Skins.
- `PhaseScreen` = Reckoning, Game over, Campaign won (opens and closes itself from the phase; the lowest priority: every other screen hides it and it returns when that screen closes while the phase holds, so the host can always press Next Night; Esc opens Pause over it). **The Intermission has no screen** (02 §3.1): the player walks the bar and presses the GO LIVE pedestal; the round result / Cash / next ask are two toasts (`PhaseScreen` queues them and holds them while another screen is open). A client waiting on the host shows "Waiting for the host · N s". `LoadingScreen` (04 "Session start and roster") is not a router modal: a root `CanvasLayer` (layer 120) with a Cancel. Game over and Campaign won offer **Back to menu** (`NetManager.leave()`) and, to the host, **New campaign** (`NetManager.start_game()`: every peer reloads `MainLevel` with a new seed). `EventVote` opens over the Intermission screen and closes on a pick, its 20 s clock, the Event applying or the phase moving on.
- `PauseMenu`: Resume · Settings · Controls (not on phones) · Leave to menu (a host with peers asks twice). It says the match keeps running. The HUD and the touch widgets stay under the dim; a toast hides while a screen is open.

**Region map** (canvas units, height 720, width ≥ 1280: a 2400×1080 phone is 1600×720; fixed, probe-checked in `tests/ui/ui_probe.gd`): two HUD groups inside `x[140, W-140] y[8, 64]`, Cash vs Quota flush left and Round clock + shots flush right · fps `x[140, 300] y[68, 96]` · turn line `x[W-440, W-140] y[68, 96]` (right-aligned under the clock) · shot clock `x[W/2±160] y[64, 104]` · toast `x[W/2±300] y[108, 152]` · call-pocket row `x[W/2±240] y[156, 228]` · host-away banner `y[232, 272]` · payout `x[240, W-240] y[576, 636]` · desktop prompt `x[W/2±200] y[H-76, H-36]` · touch: Pause `64` top corner, Back `96` top corner opposite, Jump (up-arrow icon) / Shoot / Use or Place / Drop bottom right (`x ≥ W-216`), spin disc (`120×120`) + cue-angle tile (`120×72`) bottom left, power slider right edge `x[W-96, W-24] y[108, 612]` · enlarged shot overlay card `y[108, H-24]` (HUD bar and shot clock stay visible above it) · first-time coach card `x[24, 340] y[160, ~370]` (06 P44; under the toast slot and the Back button, left of the host-away banner and the call-pocket row; on the right edge `x[W-340, W-24]` in the left-handed layout, opposite the power slider). Mirror swaps the sides. The seed lives on the F1 debug panel.

**HUD** (`HUD.tscn`, `hud.gd`; adhoc-1010a C9): only two groups, so the numbers can be big. **Cash vs Quota** (`$420 / $600`, a `FillBar` under it: cyan while filling, green once the Quota is covered, the Cash in red when negative) and **Round clock + shots left** (the shots read `Shots 9/10`: left of the Night's `shots_per_round`, never more than the total, 06 P45; before a Round starts, in the Night start and every Intermission, the clock and the shots show the next Round's full `round_time_limit_sec` and `shots_per_round`, not 0:00 / 0, from the Night's config so every peer agrees; both turn red while a Round is live at ≤ 10 s / ≤ 1 shot), a small right-aligned **turn line** under the clock while a Round is live and the table is not being staged (`Your turn` in green, else `<name> is shooting`; `WaitText.turn`, from `RoundDirector.current_shooter_id` and the roster, so every peer names the shooter), plus the payout line at the bottom (the last shot's result, 4.5 s, cleared when the local player enters CUE: a FOUL never rides into the next aim) and the optional fps. Night, Round, viewers, hype and effects are not here any more; they are on the Blackboard.

**First-time coach** (`src/ui/coach.gd`, built by the HUD as `HUD.coach`; adhoc-1014a V2, 06 P44). Replaces the Night 1 story cards on spawn. Local to each peer, never blocks input (the card ignores the mouse; only its **Skip** button, or the `H` key on desktop, takes input), on its own `CanvasLayer` (layer 2, so the Skip tap is not eaten by the look pad) and hidden under any open modal. It applies only during the player's first Intermission and Round 1 of Night 1 (a Replay lifts that), until the seen flag is set. Steps, each derived from a signal: **GO_LIVE** (Intermission, until this player is ready: a floating `Press when you're ready to play` over the GO LIVE pedestal, always on rather than on proximity because a phone has no hover, plus the card) · **WAIT** (Round live, someone else shoots: `<name> is shooting. Wait for your turn`) · **WALK** (my turn, standing: walk up to the cue ball, tap Shoot) · **AIM** (in CUE: drag to aim) · **SPIN** (optional: the spin and angle pads) · **POWER** (pull the power bar down, let go). Signals used: `GameLoop.phase_changed / state_changed / ready_changed / staging_changed`, `RoundDirector.shooter_changed`, `Player.mode_changed`, `CueController.fired`, `UIRouter.modal_changed`; the three CUE steps also read `CueController.aim_yaw / spin / elevation_deg / power` each frame (nothing emits "the aim moved"), never a timer. The per-profile flag `coach_seen` (`SaveManager.coach_seen()`, in `user://profile.json`) is set when the first shot is fired or on Skip, not on first show. **Settings > Help > Replay tutorial** clears it and restarts a coach already in a Round. `Coach.enabled` is off in a headless run so no earlier probe sees it; `tests/ui/coach_probe.gd` (Solo) and `tests/ui/coach_pair_probe.gd` (host shoots, client waits, the swap) cover it, `src/tools/screenshot_coach.gd` shoots each step.

**Blackboard** (`src/gameplay/bar/Blackboard.tscn`, `blackboard.gd`, `src/shaders/blackboard.gdshader`; instanced in `MainLevel` at `(6.95, 1.95, -3.5)` facing −x, 3.2 × 1.75 m, y 1.07–2.83). Shows, from the same mirrored signals on every peer: `NIGHT n · ROUND i/n` (`NEXT ROUND` between Rounds, `RECKONING`, game over / campaign won), `VIEWERS` and `HYPE` (hype moves in 5% steps, with a bar), the running `HAZARD` by name, the live `DRINKS` (two lines of `drink · player`, the rest counted, a neighbour's gift marked), `LAST ROUND` (`Table cleared · 140 pts · +$270`: the Round's Cash from shots, tips, fines and world interactions, never a purchase) and `TONIGHT OWE $` with `NEXT NIGHT $` (`GameLoop.ask_for`). It rewrites a line only when its text changed. The slate (eraser smears, chalk dividers, the hype bar) is one analytic shader quad; text is `Label3D` so it keeps mipmaps at range; whole board = 2 meshes + 7 labels. Three text tiers so it reads from across the room: Round i/n (0.25 m em), viewers + hype (0.23 / 0.16) and the Hazard name (0.16) are the big chalk, drinks and the last-Round / ask lines (0.09 / 0.10) the small; ≈ 7 m is the reading range, from the 10 m break spot only the header reads. `EffectManager.live_changed`, `live_drinks()` and `live_events()` feed it (03 §5). The hazard icon (`IconCatalog.hazard`) goes in the `Text/HazardRow` node, in the 0.3 m left of the name.

**Enlarged shot controls** (`SpinOverlay`, `ElevationOverlay`; adhoc-1010a C19). The corner controls (`SpinPad`, `ElevationPad`) show the value; a tap (a swipe is not one) opens the overlay, a full-screen dimmed layer under the HUD labels with a card, closed by **OK**, a tap outside the card, leaving `CUE` or any modal. *Spin*: the cue ball at 60% of the screen height, tap or drag sets the hit point (clamped to the rim), the miscue ring is drawn, the caption says `Draw 55% · Right 30%` and warns `MISCUE` / `hard English`, **Reset** centres it. *Angle*: a side view with a protractor round the ball, drag anywhere on it for whole degrees `0–80°`, **−1° / +1°** for the last degree, **Reset**; the Jump (30°) and Masse (45°) thresholds are ticked; the range the cue cannot use now (`CueController` pushes `Ball.effective_elevation(80, spin, cue_spec)` as the cap) is shaded red and the cue the shot really gets is drawn bright over the ghost of the one asked for, with the reason (`Jump shots are locked`, `hard English (60%+) stops the cue at 44°`). Both only emit through `spin_pad.changed` / `elevation.changed`, which the Player already wires to `CueController.set_spin` / `set_elevation`; desktop keys are unchanged.

**Settings** (`Settings`, static, `user://settings.cfg`, loaded at boot by `NetManager._ready`): master volume · quality Auto / High / Medium / Low (`QualityManager`: Auto = `QualityLadder`, else tier 0 / 1 / 2 pinned) · FOV 60-110 · mouse / touch-look / cue-aim sensitivity (multipliers on the Player / CueController rates, 0.25-3x) · invert Y · touch mirror · show FPS · a **Help** tab with Replay tutorial (06 P44). **Controls**: every gameplay key / mouse action (not `debug_*`, not the dormant `reset`), click-to-rebind (physical keys and mouse buttons), a clash swaps the two actions' bindings, reset to defaults; only changes from the defaults are written.

## 3. Rules & numbers
| knob | value |
|---|---|
| walk speed | `3.5 m/s` (settled), no sprint |
| touch look / aim | `0.20 °/px` walk · `0.12 °/px` aim, `×0.25` with a second finger (settled) |
| power slider | start zone = top `15%`, cancel under `3%` (settled) |
| jump | `4.5 m/s` (existing) |
| knock | `2.0 s` limp (default), `0.4 s` get-up blend, throw shove `3.5 m/s`, blast `2.5 m` / `4.5 m/s`, hard landing `7 m/s` (§2.7) |
| interact reach | `2.0 m` (same number the host validates in `WorldInteractions`) |
| enter `CUE` | shooter + all balls resting + within `cue_reach_m` of the cue ball |
| aim wobble | aim yaw sways `± max(0, cue_aim_wobble_deg − CueSpec.wobble_reduction)` degrees (`wobble_reduction` is degrees, 02 D6) at `0.8 Hz [tune]`; the intent carries the swayed yaw at release (03 §2.2 flag resolved). Stats are re-read at `4 Hz`, not per frame |
| shot clock | the host arms it each Turn (`RoundDirector.shot_clock_started(seconds)`); every peer counts down locally and the shooter sees it in `WALK` and `CUE`; at 0 the **host** records a whiff foul (no strike, shot costs 1, 02's foul viewer drop) |
| power curve (desktop hold) | existing `charge_time 0.8 s`, `decay_time 1.6 s` |
| touch power | linear in slider travel, `0..1` |

## 4. Godot implementation sketch
- `src/gameplay/player/Player.tscn` + `player.gd` — body, `Head/Camera3D`, mode state machine, interaction probe (`ShapeCast3D` sphere `2.0 m`, mask = `Interactable.LAYER`, read once per `_physics_process`). The sphere is only the broad phase: the prompt goes to an `Interactable` whose feet-to-origin distance is `≤ 2.0 m`, the host's own test, **that the player is facing** (06 P33 U8): the flattened-XZ angle between the player's yaw and the direction from the camera to the prop is at most `40°` (pitch does not count, so looking down at a prop beside you still selects it), unless the prop is within `0.6 m`; the smallest angle wins and the nearer prop breaks a tie (`1.1°`). **Hostile props** (`heckle`, `kick_stool`, `bump_table`, `throw_drink`, `flick_lights`: `WorldInteraction.hostile`) are skipped unless a Round is live, nothing is staged (`RoundDirector.staging`, 01 "Round staging") and the player is not the shooter — facts every peer knows, so a prompt never shows for a use the host would refuse on those grounds (03 §3.4); the **Shoot** button and `enter_cue` read the same truth, and out of a live Round `enter_cue` toasts `no_round`, not *balls still moving*. The counter's three props (`bar_buy`, `bar_serve`, `throw_drink`) share one prompt, *Bartender*; **Interact** opens `BartenderMenu` (06 P17, 02 §3.6), a `ui_modal` with two tabs. **Drinks**: the host's random shelf of 5 tier-framed cards (icon, name, flavour line, buff lines green and debuff lines red, price, units left; Mystery cards red with glitching text; sold-out cards stay, greyed), a Serve / Throw mode switch and a labelled crew-target row for Serve only when there is a choice (Solo and the shooter see neither, 02 §3.6); every Buy / Throw button is ≥ 48 px and greys out from mirrored Cash, stock, Round phase and night limits, and a timing refusal says when it opens (02 §3.6). **Upgrades**: the fixed ball / cue / camera / table rows with their icons (Intermission only). The panel keeps its size in every mode (02 §3.6). A Drink pick sends `WorldInteractions.request(kind, 0, {drink_id, target_peer})` and the host validates (a Serve keeps the menu open so the stock can be watched, a Throw closes it); an Upgrade asks `EconomyManager.request_purchase`. Walking out of reach closes the menu; the `UIRouter` (06 P12) owns the mouse and blocks look / move / interact while it is open, so the menu itself never touches `Input.mouse_mode`. A rig someone carries is not offered. No two other `Interactable`s sit within 1 m of each other (probe-checked), `sit` seats excepted — the facing rule tells those apart. `tests/player/interact_probe.gd` covers the facing rule on every `Bar.PROPS` entry, the hostile prompts per Round state, the Shoot button and sitting.
- `src/gameplay/cue/cue_controller.gd` — owns `CUE`: aim line, cue mesh (its tip on the spot the spin offset names, along the elevation the strike will really use — 01 §3 "Shot guide"; a puppet's cue stays the fixed stick, spin is not on the wire), the local shooter's `ShotGuide`, pullback, reads input from `TouchControls` or mouse/keys, builds the `ShotIntent`, calls `table.sim.request_shot(intent)`. The `gate` export is gone (host validates via `PhysicsSim.shot_validator`).
- `src/ui/TouchControls.tscn` + `virtual_stick.gd`, `look_pad.gd`, `power_slider.gd`, `spin_pad.gd` — each a `Control` handling `InputEventScreenTouch/Drag` by finger index in `_gui_input`; they expose values, they never call gameplay.
- Input actions added to `project.godot`: `interact`, `cue_mode`, `call_pocket_1..6` (existing `move_*`, `jump`, `shoot`, `spin_*`, `elevate_*`, `spin_reset` stay).
- `src/ui/SkinsScreen.tscn` + `skins_screen.gd`, `src/resources/skins/skin_catalog.gd` — the Skins screen and the catalog behind it.
- `src/gameplay/player/humanoid.gd` (+ `src/resources/skins/characters/*.tres`, `character_skin.gd`) — the body, §2.7; `Player` owns one (`player.humanoid`), `PlayerSlot` skins it from its roster `char_skin` (seat colour as the fallback); `PlayerSpawner` carries the host's knock entry points and the stumble tick; `src/gameplay/bar/Bartender.tscn` is the NPC (`bartender_chat.gd`, `bartender_lines.gd`, `bartender_voice.gd`, `speech_bubble.gd` make it talk); `src/gameplay/bar/Mirror.tscn` + `mirror.gd` (+ `src/shaders/mirror.gdshader`) is the wall mirror; `src/gameplay/player/lean_feedback.gd` shows the rail lean (03 §3.7).
- Performance (07): no `get_node` in `_physics_process`, UI redraws only on value change, avatars are 2 draw calls each (+ the tag), `Label3D` not a `SubViewport`, nothing simulates while a body is standing.
